# Independent acoustic queue with the updated main baseline

## Setup

2026-10-06, RTX 3090/595.71.05, vLLM 0.31.0/torch 2.13.0+cu130, MPS off. Same base `5f95115e703ffe51e03e40ae23c78ff841c3335e` plus shared HF fix `631f5573`; NAR/VAE/sampling unchanged. AR4/KV4 GiB, acoustic B1; fixed 512/768/1024/1280 frame budgets, CoT off. Main then queue, three rounds per load after warmup.

## Results

| Load | main audio/s | Queue audio/s | Change | E2EL P95 main→queue(s) | RTF P95 main→queue |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Concurrent 4 | 6.233 | 6.428 | +3.12% | 21.992→21.385 | 0.442→0.430 |
| Concurrent 8 | 6.426 | 6.829 | +6.28% | 42.057→39.844 | 0.974→0.923 |
| Continuous 16/cap 8 | 6.763 | 7.199 | +6.44% | 47.035→45.250 | 1.416→1.273 |

Continuous-load E2EL P50 fell 17.40%, P95 fell 3.79%, RTF P95 fell 10.10%. A single 1024 frame trial improved 11.178→10.830 s, without a repeatability claim. Sampled device peaks 19284/19278 MiB.

24 CPU checks and 186 complete outputs passed. Four sequential FP32 waveforms were exact; concurrent hashes matched 11/84. GPU lifecycle checks covered zero-AR progress, pending/active cancellation and reuse. Requests 5–8 entered AR while earlier requests still synthesized; final model/runner/queue states were empty.

## Scope

A fixed-length, single-device experiment with sequential runs. Gains varied by load. This run covered acoustic queuing; natural-EOS quality, online ABC/CFG, larger NAR batches, HTTP and in-flight shutdown remained outside its scope.
