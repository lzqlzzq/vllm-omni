# Fixed-input NAR isolation with concurrent AR

## Setup

RTX 3090/580.76.05; same weights, production source and 768-frame KV/noise fixture. Original 32-step midpoint/64-evaluation solver and high-priority synthesis stream. Alternate AR-idle and four active AR requests three times; repeat with a private MPS server. KV upload, conditioning prefill and receiver waiting are outside timing; VAE is omitted. Both groups use the same events/Nsight instrumentation.

## Results

| Median NAR time | AR idle | Four AR requests active | Increase |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Separate processes | 3.432 s | 5.776 s | +68.29% |
| Same processes with MPS | 3.446 s | 3.804 s | +10.40% |

MPS reduced active NAR time 34.14%; extra co-running time fell 2.344→0.359 s (84.70% reduction). All 14 FP32 latents were exact. Each replay used 21824 velocity kernels with identical configurations/counts: one eager, one capture, 62 replay evaluations.

Representative GEMM P99: idle 0.756 ms, active 3.123 ms; with MPS idle 0.751 ms, active 0.813 ms. AR still progressed; rough median throughput was 230 versus 219 token/s, approximately 5% lower with MPS. Idle polling 3.468 s (MPS 3.481 s) did not beat drain in its single trial.

## Scope

The same-GPU environment changed NAR execution time while arithmetic and transfer capacity stayed fixed. MPS changes resource sharing as well as context scheduling, leaving their individual contributions unresolved. CUDA intervals record overlap; full-song E2E and VAE/audio quality were outside the probe.
