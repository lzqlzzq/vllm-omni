# End-to-end profile: acoustic supply gaps and AR waits

## Setup

2026-10-09, H800 PCIe 80 GB/580.82.07. Restored mixed AR scheduling plus independent acoustic queue, AR32/KV32 GiB/client 64/NAR1/VAE1/window 0/fullCoT/CFG1/ABC 4096/semantic 9000. 64 matched prompts/seeds, two full warmups, MPS off. External stage/CPU/NVTX probes and Nsight graph tracing; production arithmetic/scheduling unchanged.

## Results

The 657.466 s observation window comprises 78.431 s before the first acoustic job, 473.406 s of acoustic jobs, 105.473 s between jobs and 0.157 s final delivery. Of the inter-job gaps, 105.471 s had no ready request; ready-but-not-dispatched time was only 0.00164 s. Backlog and starvation alternate: 43/64 requests also waited over 1 s in the acoustic queue.

| Observation | Time |
| --- | ---: |
| Native token-to-CPU host scope | 504.995 s, including 502.981 s event synchronization |
| YuE pending resolution | 0.933 s |
| Automatic ABC handler | 3.532 s |
| NAR prefill GPU activity intervals | 7.772 s |
| NAR velocity GPU activity intervals | 437.600 s |
| VAE GPU activity intervals | 23.772 s |

For a matched 32-row composition, logits-step interval rose 15.319→55.720 ms while acoustic work was active. Native token-return median rose 7.52→49.90 ms; YuE pending parsing remained 0.0428/0.0432 ms. Token copying itself is not the hundreds of seconds of host waiting.

All 64 timed/two warmup/four short-trace outputs passed. Profiled throughput 21.831 audio-s/s, E2EL P95 606.632 s, RTF P95 2.507. No sampled thermal slowdown; idle/active median SM1755/1215 MHz, power 284.8/346.0 W. Graph/activity envelopes leave 42.548 s uncovered, only 1.563 s while acoustic jobs are active.

## Scope

CPU scopes, GPU intervals and job wall times overlap. Graph envelopes describe activity intervals rather than SM occupancy. This single-path profile identifies experiments for separating priority, power and resource contention.
