# NAR2/VAE1 versus NAR2/VAE2

## Setup

2026-10-07, RTX 3090/595.71.05, torch 2.13.0+cu130/vLLM 0.31.0, MPS off. Same output-ownership-fixed source, AR4/KV4 GiB, NAR2 without waiting; only VAE batch 2→1 changes, retaining tiled decoding. Four shape warmups, sequential outputs, 1024/4096 frame single trials, then three rounds per concurrent load. VAE2 ran before VAE1.

## Results

| Load | VAE2 audio/s | VAE1 audio/s | Change | E2EL P95 VAE2→VAE1(s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Equal 768/concurrent 4 | 6.531 | 6.553 | +0.35% | 18.283→18.222 |
| Mixed/concurrent 4 | 6.413 | 6.388 | -0.39% | 21.429→21.510 |
| Mixed/concurrent 8 | 6.815 | 6.812 | -0.04% | 39.919→39.943 |
| Mixed/continuous 16/cap 8 | 7.209 | 7.197 | -0.16% | 45.368→45.475 |

Throughput and tail-latency differences stayed within±0.4%. Device peak fell 23678→20766 MiB, a 2912 MiB(2.84 GiB) reduction including warmup and the 4096 frame sample. VAE batching had no clear E2E benefit here; retain configurable NAR with VAE1 preferred for this packed path.

All 212 outputs passed. VAE1's sequential four and 4096 frame FP32 waveforms were exact to historical B1; the long trial actually used NAR B1. VAE2's 4096 frame waveform differed (relative L2 about 0.000333) despite matching semantic tokens. Equal-length concurrent waveform hashes matched 4/12 between the two configurations; other concurrent sets matched 12/12, 24/24, 48/48.

## Scope

Three sequential rounds; the historical NAR1 comparison used earlier runs. The peak reduction reflects this workload. Concurrent packed numerical differences, audio quality and cancellation/shutdown remained open.
