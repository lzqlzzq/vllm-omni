# Direct packed-KV writes and acoustic submission depth

## Setup

2026-10-10, H800, MPS off, AR32/KV24 GiB/client 64/NAR≤4/token 32768/window 200 ms/VAE1, fullCoT/CFG1/ABC 4096/semantic 9000. Same 64 prompts/seeds and two full warmups each. Order: original PR→direct KV writes→depth 1→original PR. Direct writes eliminate temporary per-song KV plus the packed copy; depth 1 independently changes MAX_INFLIGHT2→1. No batch/step/Nsight probes.

## Results

| Variant | Audio/s | Content TPS | RTF P95 | Device peak (GiB) | Allocated/reserved peak (GiB) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original PR 1 | 22.18880 | 754.74 | 2.40969 | 58.516 | 37.627/57.330 |
| Direct KV writes | 22.24285 | 756.58 | 2.40560 | 58.516 | 36.948/57.330 |
| Depth 1 | 22.22649 | 756.02 | 2.43993 | 70.321 | 37.911/69.135 |
| Original PR 2 | 22.05813 | 750.42 | 2.43179 | 71.817 | 38.739/70.631 |

Direct-write throughput versus bracketing runs: +0.244%/+0.837%; depth 1: +0.170%/+0.763%. The first three runs had identical ABC/returned-token sequences and audio duration; the last original run diverged. Direct writes reduced allocated peak by 0.679 GiB, while reserved/device peaks stayed unchanged in the matching-output comparison.

Direct-write CPU tests 62 passed; depth 1 state tests 12 passed. Twelve fixed-input FP32 audio pairs were exact, including short B4/mixed B3+B1/long B4, storage-alias checks and cancellation/reuse. All 256 timed requests/eight full warmups passed with zero retry/OOM and empty final states. Direct writes were retained; depth 1 was not adopted.

## Scope

Zero-copy applies to packed assembly; conditioning prefill remains, and native paged AR KV is separate. The sequential throughput differences were small. A later original-depth run also reached a high memory peak, leaving the depth contribution unresolved. Both recipes retained their recorded tuning.
