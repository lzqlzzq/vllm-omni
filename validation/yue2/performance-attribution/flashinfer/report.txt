# FlashInfer NAR kernel screening

## Setup

H800, 2026-10-10, FlashInfer 0.7.0.post 1, BF16, MPS off, OMP/MKL 1. Five shapes: B1 1024/6144, B2 4096+4096/6144+1024, B4 1024×4; prefix 2529, Q/KV heads 16/8, head_dim128. Operator tests: three warmups, five rotating-order rounds, 32 graph replays per round. Full NAR: current→FlashInfer→FlashInfer→current; three velocity warmups, one full ODE warmup and two measured solves per arm.

## Results

| Shape | SiLU current→FI (µs) | Full ODE current→FI (s) | NAR throughput change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| B1 short | 31.98→12.07 | 0.851101→0.826964 | +2.92% |
| B1 long | 177.51→118.90 | 6.461326→6.311196 | +2.38% |
| B2 equal | 235.39→158.62 | 7.638140→7.452591 | +2.49% |
| B2 mixed | 206.70→139.08 | 7.246669→7.074621 | +2.43% |
| B4 short | 119.87→79.59 | 2.997622→2.894144 | +3.58% |

All five SiLU outputs were exact against the compiled reference. Complete velocity/latent comparisons were exact for all tested shapes; 40 measured and 20 warmup ODE solves completed, each with 32 steps/64 evaluations. All ten adjacent throughput comparisons were positive. Attention alternatives had BF16 differences and mixed/slower timings; GEMM proxies had no consistent cross-shape benefit. Retain FA3/GEMM and the narrow SiLU replacement.

The successful implementation used a torch.library custom op/fake shape to preserve fullgraph compilation. Failed wrapper/JIT attempts and workspace failures were excluded; the final attention screen used 256 MiB workspace throughout.

## Scope

Each shape used one fixed input in isolated NAR, excluding AR and VAE. Both SiLU implementations were already fused. Hardware-counter attribution and E2E effects were left for subsequent experiments; full operator tables and failed attempts are in the original report.
