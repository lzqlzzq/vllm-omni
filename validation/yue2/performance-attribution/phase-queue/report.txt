# Separate ABC/semantic scheduling views: no observed E2E gain

## Setup

2026-10-08, thermally limited H800. Same worker/native KV and acoustic queue; alternate stages every 32 nonempty steps, borrow idle capacity, preserve CFG pair reservation. Original→phase queue, 64 matched prompts/seeds plus two full warmups each; AR32/KV32 GiB/client 64/NAR1/VAE1/window 0/fullCoT/CFG1/ABC 4096/semantic 9000. Instrumented diagnostic, 83°C thermal precondition.

## Results

| Metric | Original queue | Phase queue |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Audio/s | 17.950 | 17.037 |
| E2EL P95 (s) | 736.240 | 789.731 |
| RTF P95 | 3.151 | 3.404 |
| Device peak (GiB) | 65.796 | 59.390 |
| Mean projection rows | 22.185 | 16.260 |
| Projection calls | 22093 | 30167 |
| Largest acoustic gap(s) | 50.048 | 21.580 |
| Total acoustic gaps(s) | 96.447 | 117.348 |

Throughput fell 5.09%, E2EL P95 rose 7.27%, RTF P95 rose 8.04%. Mixed-stage calls disappeared, but mean batch size fell 26.71% and call count rose 36.55%. Projected elements fell 45.49%, which is not total FLOPs or elapsed time. First acoustic job arrived 15.571 s later; shortening the largest gap did not reduce total gaps.

79 CPU tests and eight GPU smoke outputs passed. CFG lifecycle exercised 16 pairs/4296 steps, cancellation/reuse and empty final states. All 128 timed requests/four warmups passed. Median SM810 MHz; thermal flags covered 92.00%/84.86% of samples.

## Scope

One sequential profiled pair with thermal and generation-trajectory differences. Rotation length, admission shares and FIFO changed together; the tested fixed policy was not adopted. A remote formatting difference was checked as AST-equivalent.
