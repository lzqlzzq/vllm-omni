# Fixed-work AR/NAR overlap versus serial execution

## Setup

2026-10-09, H800 PCIe 80 GB/580.82.07, MPS off, 350 W unchanged. Same worker/weights, AR32/KV32 GiB, NAR B1/highest-priority stream, MAX_INFLIGHT2. Freeze decode tokens/positions/KV and replay native AR graphs plus logits; contexts do not grow. NAR uses the same 6144-frame fixture and 32-step/64-evaluation solve. Four rotated-order rounds of AR-only/NAR-only/serial/overlap; setup and warmup excluded.

## Results

| AR composition | AR alone | NAR alone | Measured serial | Overlap | Median paired reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 6 ABC+26 semantic, 448 steps | 6.372 s | 6.586 s | 12.979 s | 12.549 s | 3.50% |
| 32 semantic, 416 steps | 6.622 s | 6.571 s | 13.214 s | 12.735 s | 3.62% |

All eight paired reductions were positive: mixed 2.97–3.64%, semantic 3.54–3.65%. During overlap, AR completion took 1.969×/1.923× its isolated time; NAR stream span took 1.333×/1.309×. Both paths slow each other, including pure semantic AR.

All 32 formal and two independent trace measurements had exact AR logits/NAR latents. No sampled thermal slowdown; isolated AR median SM1590–1665 MHz versus overlap 1215–1245 MHz. Separate trace confirmed serial versus overlapping activity envelopes, not simultaneous per-kernel SM occupancy.

## Scope

Fixed-position AR/NAR compute replay, excluding sampling, scheduling, token return, prefill, VAE and audio delivery. Power, resource contention and submission timing remain combined in the measured result.
