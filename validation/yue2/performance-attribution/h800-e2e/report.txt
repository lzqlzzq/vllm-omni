2026-10-10, H800 PCIe 80 GB/580.82.07, MPS off, OMP/MKL 1, unchanged 350 W limit. main=`c548a110` plus shared loading fix `631f5573`; PR is the intermediate packed-KV/FlashInfer snapshot. main→PR→PR→main, 64 matched prompts/seeds plus two complete warmups per process. All 256 measured requests and eight warmups passed, with zero allocator retries/OOM and empty final states. SM median 1245 MHz, no sampled thermal slowdown. This snapshot approximately tied main in throughput, with lower completion latency but no consistent tail-RTF improvement.
