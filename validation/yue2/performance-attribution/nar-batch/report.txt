# Repeated NAR1 versus NAR2 comparisons

## Setup

RTX 3090/595.71.05, vLLM 0.31.0/torch 2.13.0+cu130, MPS off; AR4/KV4 GiB, same source/inputs/seeds, VAE1. Four alternating-order NAR1/NAR2 pairs. Fixed 512/768/1024/1280-frame budgets; equal-length workload 768. Every process has 16 warmup outputs and 32 measured outputs. NAR2 is a maximum, with no batching wait.

## Results

| Load | Median audio/s NAR1→NAR2 | Change | E2EL P95 change | RTF P95 change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Equal 768, concurrent 4 | 6.146→6.535 | +6.32% | -6.07% | -6.07% |
| Mixed, concurrent 4 | 6.406→6.404 | -0.02% | +0.04% | +0.04% |
| Mixed, concurrent 8 | 6.820→6.826 | +0.09% | -0.10% | -0.17% |
| Mixed, continuous 16/cap 8 | 7.193→7.208 | +0.21% | -0.05% | +3.63% |

Equal-length paired throughput gains were +12.59%/+6.07%/+6.11%/+8.20% (paired median +7.15%); all four P50 latencies worsened by 0.72–4.61%. The ratio of median P50 s increased 4.22%. Mixed paired throughput changes crossed zero; continuous-load RTF P95 worsened in all pairs by 2.33–3.93%.

All 384 formal/warmup outputs plus 12 probe outputs passed. A separate probe saw equal-length batches 1+2+1, mixed 1+1+1+1; it does not describe every formal run. Device peaks: NAR1 19278–19286 MiB, NAR2 19314–19318 MiB. Keep NAR configurable, with NAR1 default.

## Scope

Four pairs on one device, using fixed frame budgets. Equal-length waveform hashes matched 6/16 concurrent outputs; packed quality acceptance remained open. The comparison is NAR1 versus NAR2 within the same implementation.
