# NAR operator profile after direct packed-KV writes

## Setup

2026-10-10, H800 PCIe 80 GB/580.82.07, MPS off, OMP/MKL 1. Real weights and production CachedNAR/BatchedCachedNAR, fixed 2529-token prefix and 6144-frame token fixture. Five single-chunk shapes; three velocity warmups and one ODE warmup before three measured 32-step/64-evaluation solves. AR/KV24 remains resident but AR/VAE do not run during measurement; separate Nsight node traces cover warm replay.

## Results

| Shape | Attention | GEMM | SiLU×gate | Full solve median(s) | NAR frames/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| B1 1024 | 31.61% | 50.05% | 7.27% | 0.849121 | 1205.95 |
| B1 6144 | 48.11% | 37.73% | 6.54% | 6.437362 | 954.43 |
| B2 4096+4096 | 42.42% | 40.67% | 7.33% | 7.645637 | 1071.46 |
| B2 6144+1024 | 45.42% | 38.65% | 6.94% | 7.218538 | 993.00 |
| B4 1024×4 | 24.55% | 54.06% | 9.26% | 2.992097 | 1368.94 |

MLP including SiLU accounts for approximately 34.04–48.34% of accumulated GPU activity. Replay intervals with no GPU activity were 3.34%/0.80%/0.68%/0.72%/1.24%. Attention dominates longer B1; MLP dominates shorter B4. Indexing/copies are secondary. FA3, compiled norm/RoPE/SiLU and CUDA Graph were already active.

All 15 measured solves and five warmups were exact to their fixed references; trace replay/eager outputs matched, and allocator retry/OOM counts were zero. A failed default-stream graph-pool startup was excluded and retained as failure evidence.

## Scope

Kernel shares sum GPU activity; frames/s measures isolated NAR throughput. The profile excludes concurrent AR, bandwidth counters and E2E/WSB evaluation. Prefill includes setup costs.

Original diagnostic launcher and `scripts/analyze.py`; retain fixed fixture, warm replay selection and RPC completion checks.
