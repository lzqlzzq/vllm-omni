# Stage-grouped logits projection: withdrawn experiment

## Setup

2026-10-08, H800; all ABC/semantic/mixed steps use index_select, separate full/narrow projections and index_copy back to the original rows. Only the YuE projection path changes. Fixed-input microbenchmark: four alternating-order rounds, eight warmups and 80 measurements each. Short E2E: new→old→new, 16 requests/two warmups per process, AR32/KV32 GiB/NAR1/VAE1/window 0/fullCoT/CFG1, ABC 512/semantic 1024.

## Results

| Rows | Original median(ms) | Grouped median(ms) | Time change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ABC 1 | 0.3992 | 0.4138 | +3.65% |
| semantic 1 | 0.07865 | 0.09256 | +17.68% |
| ABC 32 | 0.4245 | 0.4680 | +10.24% |
| semantic 32 | 0.2175 | 0.2387 | +9.78% |
| ABC 16+semantic 16 | 0.4246 | 0.6853 | +61.43% |
| ABC 4+semantic 28 | 0.4244 | 0.6558 | +54.51% |

Short E2E throughput was 24.210/24.154/23.881 audio-s/s; new versus old +0.23%/−1.13%, no consistent gain. All 84 CPU tests, 48 timed outputs, six warmups and four CFG1.5 short generations passed. Pure-stage fixed outputs were exact; mixed-stage differences passed rtol 0.015/atol 0.002 but were not exact.

Reducing projected elements did not offset extra gather/scatter and a second projection. The change was withdrawn; the restored source is not the experimental implementation.

## Scope

Synthetic projection weights and short-budget E2E requests. All 48 timed requests reached their planned stage budgets. Per-kernel overhead attribution and long-song quality remained open.
